About Me

My Background, Education, and Journey

Hello, I'm Kimberly Wang.

I am an Electrical Engineering student at the University of Pittsburgh with a strong passion for embedded systems, robotics, and digital logic design. My journey in engineering is driven by a curiosity to understand how hardware and software interact to create intelligent systems.

I enjoy taking complex problems and breaking them down into manageable components, whether it's designing a PCB for a rover or coding a finite state machine for a vending machine simulation. When I'm not in the lab, I'm constantly looking for new technologies to learn and apply to my personal projects.
