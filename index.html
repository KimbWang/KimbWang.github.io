About Me

Learn about me

San Jose, CA / Troy, NY

Hello, I'm Kimberly Wang.

I am an Electrical Engineering student at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (RPI) with a focus on hardware design. My goal is to apply these skills to innovate within the robotics and automotive industries. Outside of classes, I work as a Researcher at the Mercer XLab under ADI Fellow Douglas Mercer creating a Software Configured Breadboard (SCB) and as an Student Assistant for the 'Intro to ECSE' course.

My engineering passion is fueled by a lifelong interest in learning how things work. Starting from a young age where I learned to design tools with LEGOs, I now continue my practice of hands on work throughout my education. In my downtime, I enjoy staying active through biking or relaxing with a good movie.
